# MIRROR-CONST-001 — MirrorAI Constitution v1.0

**Product:** MirrorAI  
**Owner:** NestHive  
**Status:** Governing document

## Preamble

MirrorAI is founded on the principle that digital technology and artificial intelligence should strengthen human awareness and judgment rather than replace them. MirrorAI exists to help users see relevant digital-risk signals, understand uncertainty, verify when needed, and make their own decisions.

## Constitutional principles

1. **Human decision sovereignty.** MirrorAI assists; the user decides.
2. **Evidence before conclusions.** Risk assessments must be grounded in identifiable signals and available evidence.
3. **Explainability.** No warning or score should be presented without an understandable explanation.
4. **Uncertainty is explicit.** The system must state what it cannot verify and avoid false certainty.
5. **Risk is not confidence.** A potentially high-risk situation may still have low analytical confidence when evidence is incomplete.
6. **Privacy by design.** Process only what is needed and retain only what is justified.
7. **Data minimization.** User content must not be stored or logged by default without a defined requirement and policy.
8. **AI is a component, not the product identity.** MirrorAI must remain independent from any single model or provider.
9. **Provider outputs are not truth by default.** AI-generated analysis must be structured, validated, and governed by product logic.
10. **Safety over theatrical certainty.** MirrorAI must prefer a clear limitation over an unsupported definitive claim.
11. **Arabic-first accessibility.** The product should serve Arabic-speaking users as a first-class audience and support RTL correctly.
12. **Knowledge requires governance.** Published or AI-generated content does not automatically become approved product knowledge.
13. **Security by architecture.** Secrets and provider credentials must never be exposed through the client application.
14. **Accountability through versioning.** Important risk logic, prompts, and methodology should be traceable by version when practical.
15. **Controlled evolution.** New capabilities must not compromise the core purpose or expand the MVP without an explicit product decision.

## Core maxim

> **No Score Without Signals. No Warning Without Explanation. No Certainty Without Evidence.**

## Product promise

MirrorAI does not think instead of the human. It helps the human see more clearly before deciding.

**MirrorAI — See clearly. Decide consciously.**
